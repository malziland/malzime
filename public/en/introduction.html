A media literacy tool

One photo –
and they know it all.

Or so the AI claims – after one look at a single picture. Find out what it thinks it knows about you.

01 What happens here

Upload an image – that is all it takes. The AI reads an entire life out of it: estimated age, origin, occupation, interests, presumed buying behaviour. It even names where someone could be exploited. On top of that malziME works out what this profile would be worth on the data market, and reveals what travels along unnoticed inside the picture: camera, time and place.

Yet the machine knows nothing at all. It just sounds suspiciously certain – and that is the point where a room starts to think.

02 No show. Real AI.

No script, no prepared result, no sleight of hand. Every photo goes to a real AI model. What appears on screen comes into being while everyone watches.

That is the difference between „someone told me that…“ and „I just saw it myself“.

03 Why it lands

That data is being collected is common knowledge. It just does not touch anyone – until your own face is on the projector and a machine starts explaining who you are.

  • Knowledge turns into experience. „They collect data anyway“ is a shrug. Your own profile on the screen is a moment.
  • It gets things wrong – and judges anyway. The misfire is what lands: this is exactly how such systems decide about people they have never seen.
  • The conversation starts on its own. People compare, object, laugh – and suddenly everyone is talking about data trails, with nobody having to lecture.

06 And your data? Stays yours.

A tool that teaches about data protection must not trample on it. So:

  • No account, no tracking cookies. No sign-up, no persistent IP address, no advertising, no selling of data. The photo is deleted automatically right after the analysis – usually within seconds.
  • The location stays out. GPS details from the image never reach our servers. They are read on the device and are not sent with the upload.
  • The analysis runs in Europe. The photo goes to a French AI provider via its EU endpoint – deliberately not to one of the large US AI services.
  • And you do not have to take our word for it. The entire source code is open on GitHub – readable line by line.

Everything in detail is in the privacy policy.
