02 What malziME is – and what it is not

malziME is an educational and awareness tool. Based on an uploaded photo it shows what AI algorithms and data brokers might claim about a person.

  • All generated profiles are fictional, unproven and serve educational purposes only.
  • malziME is not a diagnostic tool, not a personality test and not an assessment of real attributes.
  • The results are not statements of fact. They merely show how algorithms could work – not what a person is actually like.

03 Audience and age recommendation

malziME is aimed at pupils, teachers, parents and anyone who wants to understand how algorithmic profiling works.

  • Under 10 years: use is not recommended. The content presumes a basic understanding of data processing and media literacy.
  • 10 to 13 years: use is recommended only with educational supervision. We recommend using the example photos rather than one's own pictures. Under § 4 of the Austrian Data Protection Act (Datenschutzgesetz, DSG), processing personal data of children under 14 requires the consent of a person with parental responsibility.
  • From 14 years: suitable for use in workshops and for raising one's own awareness independently.

04 Permitted use

You may use malziME in order to:

  • analyse your own photos, stock photos or the example photos provided,
  • use and share the results (for example PDF exports) for educational purposes.

05 Prohibited use

The following uses are expressly prohibited:

  • Misuse of the results: profiles may not be circulated as statements of fact about real people. You may not use the results to insult, discriminate against, bully or expose other people.
  • Photos of third parties without consent: do not upload photos of people who have not agreed to it – unless they are stock photos, or the use takes place within a supervised workshop with the corresponding consent.
  • Illegal content: uploading illegal content, content glorifying violence, pornographic content or child abuse material is prohibited. Mistral AI screens all requests automatically for such content.
  • Automated access: bots, scrapers or automated bulk requests are not permitted. malziME is protected by rate limiting: a guide value of roughly 500 requests per 10 minutes per IP address, plus a hard limit of 500 analyses per hour in total.
  • Circumventing protective measures: attempts to circumvent the abuse protection, rate limits or other technical safeguards are prohibited.
  • Commercial use: using malziME or its results for commercial purposes (for example paid profiling services or advertising profiles) is not permitted without prior written consent.

06 Photos and data processing

The full description of the data processing is in our privacy policy. In summary:

  • Your photo is processed briefly for the analysis only and deleted immediately afterwards – it is not stored permanently.
  • The generated profile sits on the server only briefly, until your browser collects it, and is deleted after roughly 2 hours at the latest.
  • There is no tracking, no cookies, no analytics and no advertising.
  • The AI analysis runs through Mistral AI (Paris, EU). Mistral may not use the data for its own purposes. On the paid API tier we use, there is also no AI training on inputs or outputs. Details are in the privacy policy.

You are responsible for making sure that you have the right to upload a photo.
